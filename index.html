Who I Am

About Me

I'm a graduate in Aeronautical Engineering from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka. Academics have always been something I took seriously — but some of my biggest growth has happened outside the lecture hall.

I led the MIST team at the AIAA Design/Build/Fly (DBF) Competition 2026, where we ranked 18th worldwide in Proposal and 17th worldwide in Design Report. The year before, as Manufacturing Lead and Flyoff Member, we finished 1st in Asia in flight mission scoring and 39th worldwide overall.

My undergraduate thesis focused on the thermo-mechanical behavior of anisotropic Metal Matrix Composite panels — developing a finite difference computational framework to study how composite structures respond under combined thermal and mechanical loading.

I'm working towards a career in aerospace research and education. I love problems that sit right at the edge of materials science and aerospace structures.

Thermomechanical Analysis of MMC Panels — Undergraduate Thesis
Research

Developed a finite difference computational framework to investigate thermal stress distributions in Metal Matrix Composite (MMC) panels under combined thermal and mechanical loading. Evaluated stress concentrations and failure-prone zones in anisotropic composite structures — directly relevant to aerospace structural safety and failure analysis. Supervised by Dr. Shaikh Reaz Ahmed, Professor, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, BUET.
