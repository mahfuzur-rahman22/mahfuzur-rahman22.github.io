About me

I am an aeronautical engineering graduate interested in composite materials, structural mechanics, and computational modelling.

I graduated from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka, with a CGPA of 3.80/4.00. My undergraduate thesis investigated the thermomechanical behaviour of anisotropic metal matrix composite panels, using a finite difference computational framework.

I enjoy connecting analysis with practical engineering. As team lead for MIST at the AIAA Design/Build/Fly competition in 2026, I worked across aircraft design, manufacturing, and flight testing. Our design report placed 17th worldwide and our proposal placed 18th. The previous year, I served as manufacturing lead for the team's 2025 aircraft.

My industrial training at US-Bangla Airlines introduced me to aircraft maintenance and airworthiness through work with the CAMO and AMO departments on ATR 72-600 and Boeing 737-800 fleets.

I am working towards a career in aerospace research and education, and welcome conversations about graduate study and research collaborations.
