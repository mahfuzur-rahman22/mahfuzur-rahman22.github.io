Thermomechanical Behaviour of Anisotropic Metal Matrix Composite Panels

Undergraduate Thesis  ·  Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Shaikh Reaz Ahmed, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, BUET
Problem How do thermal stress fields develop and concentrate in anisotropic Metal Matrix Composite (MMC) panels under combined thermo-mechanical loading — and where are failure-prone zones most likely to form?
Method Built a displacement-function-based finite-difference computational framework in MATLAB to solve the coupled thermoelastic field (stress, displacement, temperature) across orthotropic composite panel geometries under prescribed boundary conditions.
Outcome Solver accuracy exceeds ABAQUS FEM benchmarks under equivalent boundary conditions. Characterised stress concentration zones as functions of fibre volume fraction and temperature level — validated against ANSYS thermal–structural simulations.
